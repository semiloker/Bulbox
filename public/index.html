Tor: checking… Tor exit IPs are published, so anti-fraud services know all of them. Great for privacy, but many sites refuse or ban signups made through one — independently of which email domain you use.
Needs the Tor Expert Bundle (or Tor Browser). Point here if it isn't auto-found. Each email gets its own Tor circuit → its own exit IP.
Detecting transports…
One bridge per line (obfs4 / webtunnel / meek). Get fresh ones from Tor Browser → Settings → Connection → Bridges, or Telegram @GetBridgesBot, or email bridges@torproject.org with get transport obfs4.
Your database, the per-email browser sessions and Tor's data all live here. Copy this folder to move everything to another machine.

Per-email browser: the Browser button on each row opens an in-window browser panel (right here, like the mail panel) with its own toolbar and a private, persistent session for that email — cookies and logins are saved and isolated per email, and wiped when you delete the row.
